## 2. What Went Well

- **The port + envelope seam paid off a third time — for free.** `ConfigSource` was built by copying the `AddressProvider` shape verbatim: stub adapter, BFF route, AD-9 envelope, non-throwing `apiFetch` (`data.ok === false`), TanStack Query ownership. Story 3.1 needed almost no design — the seam is now a template. This is the pipeline-fidelity thesis at its strongest: an architectural decision, once encoded, reproduces without re-litigation.
- **Extracting every decision into a pure function made node-only tests genuinely load-bearing.** Steps 1–3 each follow container + pure-helper + pure `resolve-*-step` mapper. `filterItemsForType`, `pruneSelection`, `deriveToggledId`, `nextSelection`, `filterQuestions`, `validateAnswer`, and every view-state mapper are pure and exhaustively unit-tested. The suite tripled (137 → 293) and the growth is real coverage, not ceremony — the pure-logic lane is doing the work the SSR harness can't.
- **The two-parallel-reviewer loop caught two HIGH defects the gates could not.** Story 3.4: both reviewers *independently* converged on the prune-effect wiping a valid selection on a transient `ready→error` (reachable via 60s staleTime + failure-envelope refetch). Story 3.5: the Blind Hunter confirmed *empirically* that manual `aria-describedby` clobbered MUI's native `-helper-text` wiring, silently breaking the a11y acceptance criterion for 3 of 7 field kinds. Both are invisible to typecheck/lint/build/test. The loop is the primary defence for exactly this class.
- **Forward-compatible aggregate modelling kept the reset story true.** Story 3.2 added `type`/`items`/`details` slots to `emptyForm()`/`stepFormDefaults()`; Epic 2's `changeAddress` reset — built to rebuild from the baseline — reset all three new slots with zero edits. Story 2.5's structural intent held under Epic 3's real slots exactly as predicted.
- **The `never`-default exhaustive switch made the 7-kind renderer safe to extend.** Both `validateAnswer` and `DynamicField` switch over the `PropertyQuestion` discriminated union with a `const _exhaustive: never` default — adding an 8th question kind fails to compile until both the validator and the renderer handle it. Type-level intent enforcement, not runtime hope.

## 3. What Was Challenging

- **The prior-story defect class recurred — and a new one joined it.** The isError-before-pending mapper ordering (a real patched defect in Story 3.3, matching the Epic 2 pattern) shows the non-throwing-envelope model is a persistent foot-gun: every view-state mapper must check `isError` before the `data === undefined` pending branch or a thrown query shows an infinite spinner. New this epic: **config-derived pruning effects** (Story 3.4) that treat a transient non-ready state as a known-empty set and wrongly prune valid selections. Both are "derived state reacting to a transient" — the same family as Epic 2's stale-transient-UI class.
- **The a11y wiring gap was invisible to the whole automated suite.** Story 3.5's HIGH (`aria-describedby` clobbering MUI's helper-text association) shipped through implementation because the delivered `DynamicField.test.tsx` had *zero* error-path assertions — the exact gap the spec's Code Map had called for. It took an adversarial reviewer SSR-rendering an invalid field to catch it. Screen-reader-only correctness is a standing blind spot of node-only structural tests.
- **The node-only interaction ceiling bit harder, as predicted.** The accordion stepper is heavily interaction-driven (expand/collapse progression, completion indicators, focus/announcement). Step-progression and one-expanded-invariant behaviour rest on documented manual a11y checks plus pure `nextExpanded`/`isStepComplete` unit tests — not on rendered interaction assertions. The Epic 2 retro flagged this would intensify; it did. We chose (in the 3.2 spec) to stay node-only + extract-to-pure rather than open a jsdom lane — a deliberate spike-calibrated trade-off, now noted a fourth time.
- **MUI SSR quirks cost real debugging.** `Mui-selected` appears in emotion style rules regardless of selection state (must assert `aria-pressed`), `ToggleButtonGroup exclusive` emits `null` on re-click (must suppress for required-selection), Slider renders `type="range"` not `role="slider"`, and MUI v9 moved `inputProps`→`slotProps.htmlInput`. Each was a one-time tax, now captured in the working notes, but they slowed several stories.

## 7. Pipeline-Fidelity Verdict

Epic 3 is the strongest evidence yet that **intent survives the pipeline**. The `ConfigSource` port reproduced the Epic 2 seam with near-zero design cost; the flow aggregate absorbed three new step slots without touching reset logic; the discriminated-union content schema drove an exhaustive, type-safe 7-kind renderer end-to-end from Zod contract to rendered field. Where drift appeared it was **mechanical, not conceptual** — MUI SSR quirks, mapper ordering, an a11y wiring detail — all caught by the pure-logic suite plus the adversarial reviewer loop, never by luck. The two standing structural gaps (the un-landed boundary arch test and the node-only interaction ceiling) are known, bounded, and spike-calibrated trade-offs rather than surprises. The methodology is holding as the surface area grows.
