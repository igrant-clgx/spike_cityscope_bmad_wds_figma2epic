## Overview

**Page Purpose:** Answer 3 quick questions about the renovation (type, what to renovate, and additional details) so the tool can calculate her estimate.

**Entry Context:** Arrives from Address Entry (1.1) after entering/selecting her property address; the confirmed address is shown at the top of this page.

**Exit Action:** Completes the final question in the 3-step accordion (renovation type → what to renovate → more questions), triggering the estimate calculation and moving to the Estimate/Report Display (1.3).

**On-Page Interactions:**
- Step 1: Renovation type — select Internal or External
- Step 2: What to renovate — selection options
- Step 3: More questions — additional detail inputs
- Accordion expand/collapse behavior between steps (per Figma reference)
- "Enter new address" link to go back and change the address without leaving the flow

## Page States

|-------|------|------------|---------|

| Step 1 Answered | Internal/External selected | Step 1 auto-collapses showing selection (summary retained); Step 2 unlocks and auto-expands | Answer Step 2 |
