## Overview

**Page Purpose:** See the calculated renovation cost estimate for the property, presented clearly enough that Hannah feels confident using it in her decision.

**Entry Context:** Arrives from Questionnaire (1.2) after completing all 3 steps (renovation type, what to renovate, more questions).

**Exit Action:** Reviews the estimate; scenario complete ✓. (Possible future action: revise inputs, or share/save the report — out of scope for v1, static data only.)

**On-Page Interactions:**
- Displays the property address and calculated cost estimate (static/mock value for v1)
- No further action required for v1 — no lead capture, contact request, or quote follow-up
