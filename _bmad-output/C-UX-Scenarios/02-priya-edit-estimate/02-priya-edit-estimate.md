## Transaction (Q1)

Looking at her range, Priya explores "what if I drop the bathroom?" and gets a revised estimate — without re-entering anything she already answered.

---

## Business Goal (Q2)

**Goal:** Keep Priya engaged and in control so she trusts the tool enough to convert.
**Objective:** Objective 1 (fast, low-friction) + Objective 3 (defensible, explorable estimate).

---

## User & Situation (Q3)

**Persona:** Priya (PRIMARY ⭐)
**Situation:** She has a result on screen and is curious how one scope change moves the number.

---

## Driving Forces (Q4)

**Hope:** To tweak one answer and instantly see the effect.
**Worry:** That editing means starting the whole form over.

---

## Device & Starting Point (Q5 + Q6)

**Device:** Desktop / Mobile.
**Entry:** Already on the Results view from Scenario 01.

---

## Best Outcome (Q7)

**User Success:** Changes one item and sees a revised range on the same page, with all other answers preserved.
**Business Success:** Higher trust and dwell time; she stays in the flow rather than abandoning.

---

## Shortest Path (Q8)

1. **Results view** — taps **Edit Estimate** (secondary); the form returns to Step 1 with **every answer preserved**.
2. **Step 2 — What to renovate** — reopens it, deselects **Bathroom**.
3. **Get Estimate** — submits; a revised range appears on the same page. She never re-enters address or Step 1. ✓
