- (decision) Voice/tone: plain, honest, low-pressure AU homeowner. Not salesy. E.g. 'Roughly what your renovation could cost' not 'Get your FREE quote now!'. Disclaimer always visible; estimate framed as a range with confidence.

- (decision) Interaction primitives: click/tap primary; full keyboard operability; visible focus ring (accent) on every interactive el; accordion headers = buttons w/ aria-expanded; Enter/Space toggles selection; Esc not modal-heavy (inline flow). Debounced address autocomplete (>=300ms).
