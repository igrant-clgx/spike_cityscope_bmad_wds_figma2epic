# Marcus the Property Investor / Agent — Secondary Persona

> SECONDARY target — the repeat, address-swapping power user who stress-tests the flow's speed

**Priority:** SECONDARY 💼
**Role in Flywheel:** Uses the calculator repeatedly across properties; validates efficiency and safe address change
**Created:** 2026-08-13

> ⚠️ Derived from `product-brief.md §3` and `EXPERIENCE.md` — **pending validation with real users.**

## Profile Summary

**Marcus evaluates renovation potential across many properties.** For him the calculator isn't a once-off — it's a quick tool he reaches for again and again as he sizes up deals. He's more digitally fluent than Priya and less emotionally cautious, but he's ruthless about his time: anything slow, repetitive, or unreliable loses him instantly. He matters because he pushes the flow's *repeat-use and address-change* mechanics harder than anyone.

---

## Current Situation

**The Daily Struggle:**
- Needs fast renovation-cost signals across a shifting set of addresses
- Re-entering the same answers for each property is tedious
- Can't trust a number vague enough to be useless in a decision

**Skills & Tools:** Comfortable with property tools and data; high expectations for speed and reliability.

**The Gap:** A fast, repeatable estimate where changing the address is frictionless and predictable.

---

## Psychological Profile
- Efficiency-driven, pragmatic, numbers-oriented
- Trusts tools that are transparent about method and consistent across runs
- Low tolerance for friction, surprises, or hidden state changes

---

## Driving Forces

### ✅ Top 3 Positive Drivers (What He Wants)

**1. Re-run estimates quickly across many addresses**
- **Product Promise:** Fast flow + "New Estimate" reset and "Edit Estimate" (preserves answers) actions. (`EXPERIENCE.md § Component Patterns`.)

**2. Change the property address without losing his place**
- **Product Promise:** "Enter new address" change modal with explicit Confirm/Cancel; focus returns to trigger. (`EXPERIENCE.md`.)

**3. Trust the range enough to act on it**
- **Product Promise:** Range + "how calculated" explainer + confidence signal; consistent method across runs (G3).

### ❌ Top 3 Negative Drivers (What He Fears)

**1. Slow, repetitive re-entry** → **Product Answer:** Edit preserves answers; reset is one action; minimal required inputs.

**2. Address change silently corrupting prior answers** → **Product Answer:** Changing a confirmed address deliberately resets dependent Step 1–3 scope, via an explicit modal — no silent, ambiguous state. (`[ASSUMPTION]` OI-7 — the keep-vs-reset rule is a carried open item to confirm.)

**3. An estimate too vague to use** → **Product Answer:** Bounded range with confidence + method transparency, not a hand-wave.

---

## Validation Strategy

**What Marcus needs to see:**
1. Consistent, transparent method (so numbers compare across properties)
2. Obvious, safe address switching
3. Speed on repeat runs

## Conversion Path
- **Discovery:** finds it via Demo Channel or a property context
- **Evaluation:** runs it against a property he already knows to sanity-check the range
- **Adoption:** uses it as a quick first-pass filter across deals
- **Advocacy:** recommends it to peers if it's fast and consistent

---

## Impact on Business Goals
- Drives **repeat usage** and exercises the address-change + reset paths that keep the flow robust.
- A secondary (not primary) lead source, but a strong reliability/efficiency signal for the whole design.
