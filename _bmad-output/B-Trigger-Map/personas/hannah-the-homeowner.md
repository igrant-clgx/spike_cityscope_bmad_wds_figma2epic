# Hannah the Homeowner - Primary Persona

> Primary target - the direct end-user whose experience the entire product is built around

**Priority:** PRIMARY 🏠
**Role in Flywheel:** Uses the tool to get a fast, trustworthy renovation cost estimate — her confidence in the result is the foundation the whole product depends on.
**Created:** 2026-08-12

---

## Profile Summary

Hannah is in her mid-30s to 50s, either living in a property she's considering renovating or eyeing a new home before purchase. She's not a design or construction expert — she just wants a realistic sense of "what would this cost me?" before she gets emotionally attached to a listing or commits to a renovation project.

She matters to the product's success because she is the primary, direct user of the flow we're building (address entry → questionnaire → estimate). If Hannah trusts and completes this flow, the product succeeds — Aiden's value (a credible report to support listings) is a natural byproduct of Hannah's experience being solid.

---

## Current Situation

### Daily Life

**The Daily Struggle:**
- Busy juggling work and family, browsing property listings in spare moments on her phone or laptop
- Frustrated by vague or inconsistent contractor quotes
- Anxious about not knowing if a fixer-upper is a smart buy or a money pit
- Tired of slow back-and-forth just to get a rough number

**Skills & Tools:**
- Comfortable with everyday web/mobile tools, not a construction or finance expert
- Currently relies on guesswork, a tradie's rough quote, or skips analysis altogether

**The Confidence Gap:**
- She needs a fast, no-pressure way to get a ballpark renovation cost
- Without it, she either overpays for expert advice, waits too long, or makes a decision blind

## Psychological Profile

### Personality & Motivations

**Core Identity:**
- Practical and cautious with money
- Wants to feel informed and in control of a major life decision
- Values transparency over sales pitches

**Approach:**
- Prefers self-serve tools she can use on her own time
- Wants clear, simple answers — not jargon or vague ranges she has to decode

---

## Driving Forces

### ✅ Top 3 Positive Drivers (What She Wants)

**1. Confidence before a big decision**
- Wants to feel informed and in control before committing to a purchase or renovation
- Matters because this is often one of the biggest financial decisions of her life
- Success looks like: walking away from the tool feeling "I know what I'm getting into"
- **Renovation Calculator Promise:** A clear, simple cost estimate presented right after a short questionnaire, with no ambiguity about what it means.

**2. An effortless answer**
- Wants a quick result without chasing down contractors for quotes
- Matters because her time is limited and contractor coordination is slow and frustrating
- Success looks like: getting an estimate in a few clicks, no phone calls needed
- **Renovation Calculator Promise:** A ≤3-step flow (renovation type → what to renovate → more questions) that produces an estimate immediately.

**3. A sense of control**
- Wants to feel like she's driving the process, not at the mercy of tradespeople
- Matters because it reduces the feeling of vulnerability in a high-stakes decision
- Success looks like: being able to explore different renovation scenarios herself
- **Renovation Calculator Promise:** A self-serve tool she can revisit and adjust inputs on her own.

---

### ❌ Top 3 Negative Drivers (What She Fears)

**1. Buying a "money pit"**
- Fears discovering hidden renovation costs after she's already committed to a property
- This terrifies her because it can turn a dream home into a financial burden
- Failure looks like: being blindsided by costs well beyond what she estimated
- **Renovation Calculator Answer:** Present the estimate clearly upfront, before a purchase decision is finalized, based on the specific renovation type and scope she selects.

**2. Vague quotes anxiety**
- Fears getting inconsistent, unclear numbers from different contractors
- This bothers her because it makes her feel she can't trust any single number
- Failure looks like: three contractors giving three wildly different quotes with no explanation
- **Renovation Calculator Answer:** A structured, consistent estimate based on clear inputs (renovation type, scope), reducing perceived randomness.

**3. Overpaying / being taken advantage of**
- Fears being charged more than a renovation should reasonably cost
- This worries her because she lacks the expertise to independently verify pricing
- Failure looks like: paying well above market rate without knowing it
- **Renovation Calculator Answer:** An independent, neutral-feeling estimate she can use as a reference point before engaging contractors.
