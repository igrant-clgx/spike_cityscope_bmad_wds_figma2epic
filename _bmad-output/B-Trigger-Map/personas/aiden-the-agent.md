# Aiden the Agent - Secondary Persona

> Secondary target - benefits from a strong Hannah-focused experience, using it to support his listings

**Priority:** SECONDARY 💼
**Role in Flywheel:** Shares the report/tool with buyers to remove hesitation and support a listing, benefiting indirectly from Hannah's trust in the tool.
**Created:** 2026-08-12

---

## Profile Summary

Aiden is a real estate agent, mid-career, juggling multiple listings and client relationships. His day is fast-paced — property inspections, client calls, prepping listing materials, following up on leads.

He matters to the product's success as the secondary audience: a tool that gives homeowners (his buyers) confidence also gives him a credible asset to support his listings and close deals faster. His needs are addressed as a byproduct of the primary Hannah-focused design, with secondary attention on trust and differentiation.

---

## Current Situation

### Professional Reality

**The Daily Struggle:**
- Buyers hesitate or walk away when they can't picture renovation cost/value on a property
- A stalled or lost deal costs him time and commission
- Competing agents may offer better-supported listings

**Skills & Tools:**
- Comfortable using digital tools and links to support listings
- Currently either commissions slow, costly professional reports or simply reassures buyers verbally with no data to back it up

**The Credibility Gap:**
- He needs a fast, credible-looking report he can attach to a listing
- Without it, he risks losing deals to hesitation or to competitors with better tools

## Driving Forces

### ✅ Top 3 Positive Drivers (What He Wants)

**1. Differentiate his listings**
- Wants his listings to stand out from competing agents' listings
- Matters because buyers have many choices and a distinguishing feature helps win attention
- Success looks like: buyers commenting on how helpful his listing materials are
- **Renovation Calculator Promise:** A shareable estimate/report tied to a listing that other agents may not offer.

**2. Build trust with buyers**
- Wants buyers to see him as credible and data-driven, not just persuasive
- Matters because trust reduces buyer hesitation and speeds up decisions
- Success looks like: buyers referencing the estimate as a reason they felt comfortable proceeding
- **Renovation Calculator Promise:** A neutral-feeling, structured estimate that reads as independent rather than a sales pitch.

**3. Close deals faster**
- Wants to remove buyer hesitation caused by uncertainty about renovation costs
- Matters because faster closes mean more commissions and less time wasted
- Success looks like: fewer stalled deals due to renovation cost uncertainty
- **Renovation Calculator Promise:** An instant estimate buyers can access themselves, removing a common source of delay.

---

### ❌ Top 3 Negative Drivers (What He Fears)

**1. Looking unprepared**
- Fears being unable to answer a buyer's renovation cost questions on the spot
- This bothers him because it undermines his professional credibility
- Failure looks like: a buyer asking a renovation cost question he can't answer
- **Renovation Calculator Answer:** A tool he can point buyers to directly, giving them (and him) an immediate answer.

**2. Losing a sale**
- Fears losing a deal because a buyer couldn't picture the renovation cost/value of a property
- This worries him because it's a direct hit to his income and reputation
- Failure looks like: a buyer walking away from an otherwise good listing
- **Renovation Calculator Answer:** Removes a key point of buyer hesitation by giving them clarity early in the process.

**3. Losing to competitors**
- Fears a competing agent's better-supported listing winning over his buyer
- This worries him because differentiation is a competitive necessity in his market
- Failure looks like: a buyer choosing a competitor's listing because it offered more clarity
- **Renovation Calculator Answer:** Gives him a modern, differentiated tool to offer that competitors may lack.
