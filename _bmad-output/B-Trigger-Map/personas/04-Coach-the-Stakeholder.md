# The Home Loan Coach / Demo Channel — Tertiary (Stakeholder) Persona

> TERTIARY target — the business stakeholder who consumes the leads the flow produces

**Priority:** TERTIARY 🏠
**Role in Flywheel:** Receives qualified, contextualised leads and converts them into helpful financing conversations
**Created:** 2026-08-13

> ⚠️ Derived from `product-brief.md §3,§6` and `EXPERIENCE.md` — **pending validation with the Demo Channel team.**

---

## Profile Summary

**The Home Loan Coach is the business end of the flywheel.** They (and Demo Channel behind them) don't touch the calculator UI — they receive what it produces: leads. Their success depends entirely on lead *quality* and *context*. A name and number with no scope is a cold call; a lead that arrives with the renovation scope, an estimate range, and explicit consent is a warm, effective conversation. They matter because they define what "a good lead" means, which shapes what the design must capture.

---

## Current Situation
- Depends on inbound leads to start financing conversations
- Poorly-contextualised leads waste time and convert poorly
- Operates under strict AU privacy/consent obligations

**The Gap:** A steady stream of high-intent, well-contextualised, consent-clean leads timed to the moment of homeowner interest.

---

## Psychological Profile
- Outcome-focused: cares about conversion and compliant follow-up
- Values context and intent signals over raw lead volume
- Risk-averse on privacy and consent

---

## Driving Forces

### ✅ Top 3 Positive Drivers (What They Want)

**1. High-intent leads with scope + budget context**
- **Product Promise:** Lead is created *after* a completed estimate, carrying the renovation scope and range for context. (`product-brief.md §5.1`; `EXPERIENCE.md § Component Patterns`.)

**2. Clean, explicit marketing consent**
- **Product Promise:** Lead form gates submit on required fields **and** explicit consent; no dark patterns. (`EXPERIENCE.md`; AU Privacy Act.)

**3. To reach the homeowner at the right moment**
- **Product Promise:** The coach offer appears right after the estimate — peak intent — via a phone CTA (`Call us: 0800 269 4663`) and/or lead form.

### ❌ Top 3 Negative Drivers (What They Fear)

**1. Low-quality / low-intent leads** → **Product Answer:** "Trust before ask" filters for genuine intent — only homeowners who complete the estimate and choose to engage become leads.

**2. Consent / privacy non-compliance** → **Product Answer:** Explicit consent checkbox, disclaimer gate, AU Privacy Act retention (24 months), encryption in transit/at rest.

**3. Missing scope context (cold calls)** → **Product Answer:** Estimate scope travels with the lead so the first conversation is informed.

---

## How the Coach Discovers Value
- **Experience the Difference:** leads arrive with scope + consent, not just contact details
- **Recognition:** conversion and call quality improve
- **Appreciation:** the calculator becomes a trusted top-of-funnel asset
- **Word of Mouth:** Demo Channel invests further in the touchpoint

---

## Impact on Business Goals
- **Objective 2 (> 15% leads):** defines lead quality thresholds and consent capture.
- **Objective 3 (defensible estimate):** relies on the range being credible enough to justify outreach.
- Owns the carried `[OPEN]` decision **OI-10** — phone-CTA vs lead-form as the authoritative conversion path.
